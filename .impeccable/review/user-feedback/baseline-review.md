## material_fixes

1. Fidelity / controls: recompose the desktop HUD into compact mission, selected-unit, and construction groups; remove repeated framing, empty slot boxes, redundant status symbols, and oversized map name blocks while keeping selection, charge, legal actions, and discoverable help visible.
2. Fidelity / material: replace the rejected grass, sand, and water surface treatment with quiet broad color planes and sparse water marks; retain bright role colors and raised coast geometry, then update active design guidance to this new direction.
3. Fidelity / projection: make Scout and Scoop poses share the terrain's projection and a stable physical support plane; derive or rectify views to that measured basis, calibrate each view's origin/footprint, and inspect stationary endpoints in all four headings. Translating a picture alone will not fix its camera angle.
4. Truth / resources: define separate colored part identities and carry them consistently through construction costs, loose piles, transport, dismantling, and pictured counts; do not present a mixed-color `alloy` thumbnail as distinct resources.
5. Truth / Scoop: restrict Scoop to Move, Dig, and Fill in the visible controls, targeting transitions, and underlying order validation; keep its single dirt load represented as a bucket state rather than generic pickup/drop cargo.
6. Fidelity / grounding: center the complete rock support hull on its blocked tile and fit that footprint to the tile; align hover/selection geometry with the same support plane.
7. Truth / freighters: remove boats from the current missions' earned scenery until an authored mission features them; if retained in a later marine mission, validate the full hull against a one-tile water footprint, a water-only home, and the shoreline at its largest bob pose.
8. Fidelity / coast geometry: render the exposed lower land face or an equal-depth bridge abutment at land/bridge transitions so the attachment has no visible blue gap; assess the seam from both front-facing coast directions.

## keep
