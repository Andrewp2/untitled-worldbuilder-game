# Toy world visual direction

Mode: Experience. Desktop board with a compact native bottom toolbox, menu, and always-visible pictured blueprints.

The user’s earlier [approved study](mocks/toy-world-study.png) remains the silhouette/material reference. The [September 30 feedback contract](user-feedback-brief.md) supersedes illustrated rovers/rocks, textured terrain, sidebar/mobile controls, Scoop cargo, and premature freighter scenery. Harbor Run now features playable one-tile boats with earned water residents after completion. Quiet terrain planes remain a reversible trial with final preference unresolved. The [campaign-rules contract](campaign-rules-brief.md) adds ordered access, finite stock, a short objective, legged bots and smoke within the same world. Authored levels and desktop controls govern composition.

Quality bar: original fitted toy machines with visibly different roles, saturated lime/orange/cobalt/yellow/red bodies, readable eyes, rounded leaf trees, chunky stones, cyan water and raised grassy coasts against deep blue. The Scout is quick and narrow, the Hauler has a broad empty cargo bed, the Warden has thick blue armor, Scoop is a compact yellow loader with turquoise arms/hubs and a blue rear battery, and the Bristleback has a red segmented animal silhouette. Carry the same pictures into blueprints, recipes, parts and cargo, keeping long instructions optional.

Presentation motion gives each grid step a short hop, landing at both endpoints; Scoop lifts 2 world pixels. Bodies and cargo lift while their footprint, depth, and shadow remain grounded; idle bodies stay planted. Successful terrain work gives Scoop a 3-pixel dip and dirt puff over .4 seconds. Flag cloth flutters around a fixed mast. A goal star rises with a chime before the completion choices, with a short static reward for reduced motion and an immediate skip. Question marks bob above fixed shadows. Earned residents patrol the map; authored delivery or original-unit arrival bonuses put stars on the flags after the current visit's main is complete. Reduced motion removes decorative travel and the work dip/puff, and pause freezes the current mission pose and work beat. Selection, battery charge, destinations and hazards stay readable.

Current models: all unit/enemy roles, batteries, rocks, trees, and four colored pieces are authored Three.js geometry rendered once into Phaser canvases through one 30-degree orthographic camera. Warden, Arborbot and Mender use four jointed walking legs with planted feet; vehicles retain wheels. Rover/Bristleback/rock/tree canvases are 384×384, use origin (192,280), and display at 96 world pixels; wheels and feet meet the support plane and the complete rock support hull fits the tile projection. Camera-derived bed/bucket anchors position loads at every heading. HUD thumbnails crop transparent margins without changing world origins. Trees have six broad canopy lobes and softer greens, sharing model daylight and planted origins at both sizes. Loose and installed batteries share blue cylinder, collar and cap geometry, retaining empty-cell tint and charge strips. Retained generated flag, relay, soil, and connector use calibrated cutout origins. The [manifest](../public/art/toy-world/manifest.json) retains 34 provenance PNGs; superseded model/terrain/freighter pictures remain references.

Reference: `.impeccable/mocks/toy-world-study.png` and its approval JSON. The user pin overrides direction seed d9955f0d. The direction contract is the opening body comment in `index.html`. Review evidence is `.impeccable/review/toy-world/evidence.md`.

Authored rover and Bristleback geometry keeps details consistent across headings, preserving the corrected rear-heading mapping. Bristleback has six articulated legs, a segmented red shell, three ivory spines and a grumpy blunt snout. No LEGO marks, studs, figures, or copied models are used.

Desktop controls: full board, small mission/menu/camera groups, compact bottom pictured blueprints/name/charge/actions. Blueprints stay visible; units are selected on the board or with 1–9. Installed charge uses one green bar, with exact charge on hover and for assistive technology; battery replacement appears when available. Invalid actions clear transient status and use the existing error cue, honoring effects mute; success and reward messages expire after 3.5 seconds. Costs appear on hover/focus/selection; map names and pile counts appear on hover/focus; empty cargo slots hide. Four red/blue/yellow/green quantities stay distinct through recipes, cargo, recovery, and deliveries. Scoop exposes Move/Dig/Fill only and rejects pickup/drop atomically; dirt sits in the rendered bucket. Quiet broad terrain is code art with sparse ripples, water 10px below land, solid 10px banks beside bridge ends, and 4px bridge decks on narrow posts over visible water. Ripples render beneath decks and posts. Only Hollow starts unlocked, followed by Bramble, Siltwater, Rough, Woodland, Tidepool, Harbor and Ancient; every preceding main is required and bonus stars never gate progress. One short objective replaces the mission information panel, with unit details and How to play still available. Each visit's bonus stays hidden until its main completes, including replay; the dialog offers World map or Try bonus. Finite per-model blueprint counts are visible, successful builds consume one, failure consumes none and zero is disabled. Dismantling/death never refunds stock; fresh visits/restarts restore terrain, units, materials and authored stock. Construction remains immediate, with .7-second smoke for build/dismantle/destruction; pause freezes it and reduced motion uses a .25-second static puff revealing underlying parts. Harbor completion earns Tugboat/Freighter residents; Ancient Valley earns one Mender, while the other missions earn two residents. Both music and effects start off on every load, regardless of old saved preferences, with separate opt-in for the current tab's session.

Reset progress is a small native control beneath Game menu's icon row, using the existing navy/yellow palette and restart symbol. **Hold 3s** changes to **Keep holding…** with a linear bottom meter during a continuous three-second pointer or keyboard hold; interrupted holds cancel. Confirmation clears awards and earned residents and returns to the world map.
