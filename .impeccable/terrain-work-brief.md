# Terrain-work extension

Mode: Experience, preserving the approved glossy toy world and sparse native HUD.

The user authorized a terrain-shaping machine and one handcrafted mission requiring a dirt crossing. Scoop is a compact yellow loader with turquoise arms/hubs and the same headlight eyes, four directional pictures, grounded contact, and a low step bounce. One physical load of dirt moves from open land into its bucket and into water. Native Dig / Fill actions appear only for Scoop; Space chooses according to bucket contents. Hover previews and messages explain invalid targets, while long instructions stay in details/help.

First viewport: existing island canvas and camera, new Siltwater Reach pin on the world map; inside the mission, Scoop and Scout start on the west bank of a visible two-cell river, with an unreachable flag and gold delivery pad on the far bank. Raster pictures are reused in roster, blueprints, cargo and world residents. Work produces a short dip and dirt puff while keeping the footprint/shadow planted. Pause freezes mission motion; reduced motion removes the dip/puff. Success uses the existing star/chime/completion choices and earns map residents.

Authority: DESIGN.md, .impeccable/toy-world-brief.md and .impeccable/mocks/toy-world-study.png. This is an ordinary code-led extension, not a new art world or screen-layout comp. New asset prompts and sidecars: public/art/toy-world/scoop.prompt.txt, soil.prompt.txt, scoop-{se,sw,nw,ne}.json and soil.json. No original code or sprites were copied.

Contract: conserve land plus carried/loose dirt; forbid work beneath units, reserved friendly routes, piles, flags and bonus pads; validate before queueing and again at arrival; action consumes three battery charge; dig produces water, fill produces grass; pristine authored grids survive replay. Exact energy costs are provisional game tuning.

Evidence: .impeccable/review/terrain-work/. Finish requires native browser playback, desktop/mobile/user captures, focused tests, build, bounded fresh review and documentation.
