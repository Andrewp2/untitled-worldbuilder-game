# Campaign progression and level feedback

Mode: Experience. A refinement of the approved toy world and desktop controls.

The September 30 play feedback asks for sequential mission unlocks, one main objective followed by one bonus, finite blueprints, less mission text, more legged bots, smoke when units break apart, and puzzles that combine mechanics. These requests supersede the prototype's immediately available missions, multiple main flags, pre-completion bonuses, and reusable blueprints. Existing known saved awards remain; every earlier main mission must be complete before entering a later one. Bonuses never gate the next mission.

The visible world map retains its island and pins, with small order numbers, darker stationary lock symbols and muted unopened paths. Only the next mission and earlier replays are available. Normal mission play shows a short objective, with no mission information box. The bonus marker, ground star and text appear only after the current visit's main completion; the completion dialog offers World map and Try bonus. Unit information and How to play remain available.

Each mission authors finite per-model blueprint counts. Successful construction spends one, failure spends none, and wrecks or dismantling never refund it. The pictured drawer displays remaining counts and disables exhausted choices. Fresh visits/restarts restore authored stock. Physical parts and remaining battery charge stay conserved.

Rough Ridge's gray crossing is rough ground, accessible to Trailbuggy and inaccessible to Crab, Snail and Warden. Its main objective is Trailbuggy at Lookout. Its revealed bonus is the original Snail at the far-bank star, requiring a three-cell land crossing and an optional armed escort from limited Scoop/Warden kits. Woodland Workshop requires two tree relocations through a narrow corridor, then opens a blocked supply pocket for a cargo bonus. Tidepool separates Frog's main arrival and the original Duck's bonus return. Harbor uses Freighter's arrival as its single main objective, then shore delivery. Bramble and Ancient Valley require their hostiles cleared as well as arrival at the single main flag.

Warden, Arborbot and Mender use jointed legs and planted feet, keeping their existing bodies, palette, shared camera, cargo anchors and four headings. Cars and work vehicles retain wheels. Code-drawn smoke billows during construction, dismantling and destruction; recovered piles fade into view beneath it. Smoke uses the presentation clock and stops while paused; reduced motion uses a short static puff.

No visual-world replacement, new direction seed, approved control comp or generated raster is involved. The approved toy study and user-feedback brief remain the art authority. The current music stays off by default; this task does not compose new music or modify picking, whose reported concern the user retracted.
